Where firms quietly lose cases.

Almost none of it happens during business hours, and almost none of it shows up in a report. The call simply never becomes a file.

Nights & Weekends

Voicemail after hours

Legal problems start on a Friday night as often as a Tuesday morning. A voicemail box asks a distressed caller to wait — so they hang up and dial the next firm on the results page.

During Hearings

Nobody free to answer

When your team is in court, at a deposition, or already on a call, inbound inquiries stack up. By the time someone calls back, the caller has usually already retained.

Wrong Fit

Time lost to bad matters

Consultation slots are finite. Filling them with matters outside your practice areas, or ones you would decline anyway, costs the same hour a signed case would have taken.

It stays firmly on the intake side.

The fastest objection we hear is about professional responsibility, and it is the right question to ask. The receptionist is scoped to scheduling and information gathering — the boundaries are configured explicitly, and your attorneys keep every judgment call.

NEVER

Gives legal advice. It does not interpret law or comment on the merits of a matter. Asked for advice, it defers to the attorney consultation and returns to booking.

NEVER

Quotes fees or terms. Pricing, retainers, and engagement terms are attorney conversations. It will not improvise a number.

NEVER

Forms a relationship. It schedules consultations. It does not accept matters, confirm representation, or imply either.

ALWAYS

Hands off to your attorneys. Intake goes straight into your firm's own calendar and inbox over encrypted connections, with the screening decision left entirely to your team.

Questions firms ask us.

The professional responsibility questions come first, and they should.

Does the AI give legal advice to callers?

No. It is configured to gather intake information and schedule consultations only. It never interprets law, never comments on the merits of a matter, and never quotes fees. If a caller asks for advice, it says an attorney will cover that on the consultation and moves on to booking.

How does it handle conflict checks?

It captures the details a conflict check needs at first contact — caller name, opposing party, and matter type — and passes them to your team before the consultation is confirmed. The screening decision stays with your attorneys; the AI only collects the inputs.

What happens with confidential information a caller shares?

Intake data is transmitted over encrypted connections and delivered straight into your firm's own systems — your calendar and your inbox. We configure what is captured and what is deliberately not captured, so sensitive detail is left for the attorney consultation.

Can it route by practice area?

Yes. It identifies the matter type from the conversation and routes accordingly — booking with the right attorney, flagging matters you do not take, and referring out cases outside your practice areas using rules you define.

How long does setup take?

Typically under two weeks. We start with a short discovery call covering your practice areas and intake rules, build and train the receptionist on your firm, then deploy and test it on your site before it takes a single live inquiry.
